# Data sources

`nigerian_universities_faculties.csv` is a copy of the university register from
[reality-farouqk/nigeria-tertiary-institutions-api](https://github.com/reality-farouqk/nigeria-tertiary-institutions-api)
(commit `0f4c211`), published under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication). It lists 312 Nigerian universities with
category (Federal/State/Private), location, year established, website and faculties.

Only **institution names, states and websites** are used from this file. Everything else in SDOS is synthetic:
beneficiaries, bank references, academic calendars (illustrative dates), verification outcomes and payments.
Calendar dates attached to real institutions are invented for demonstration and are not their actual calendars.
